# Furniture model credits

## Wabi-sabi set: `wabi/`

Modelled for this app in Blender 5.2 by the scripts in `tools/blender/wabi/`, procedurally and at real-world size. They're original models, not scans or copies of any product.

Materials use CC0 textures from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/textures), converted to neutral grey detail maps so the app can tint them:

| Texture | Poly Haven source | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `oak_*` | [White Oak Veneer](https://polyhaven.com/a/white_oak_veneer) | All wood (tinted by the piece's finish) |
| `linen_*` | [Rough Linen](https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_linen) | Upholstery, headboards, throws, bedding |
| `boucle_*` | [Curly Teddy Natural](https://polyhaven.com/a/curly_teddy_natural) | Bouclé armchair |
| `jute_*` | [Hessian 380](https://polyhaven.com/a/hessian_380) | Rugs, paper-cord chair and stool seats |
| `cotton_*` | [Cotton Jersey](https://polyhaven.com/a/cotton_jersey) | Mattress |
| `plaster_*` | [Plastered Wall](https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_wall) | Plaster tables, vanity counter |
| `enso_color.jpg` | Generated by `tools/blender/make_art.py` | Wall art |

## Poly Haven scans (CC0)

From [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/models), released as **CC0** (public domain; no attribution required). Packed into single `.glb` files with colour textures capped at 2048 px and other maps at 1024 px (both at 1024 px for the plants).

| File | Poly Haven model | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `wooden_display_shelves_01.glb` | [Wooden Display Shelves 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/wooden_display_shelves_01) | Bookshelf |
| `potted_plant_01.glb` | [Potted Plant 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/potted_plant_01) | Plant (tall) |
| `potted_plant_02.glb` | [Potted Plant 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/potted_plant_02) | Plant (small) |
