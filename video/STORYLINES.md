# Redhill Peaks Studio: three storylines

The one thing: **a flat HDB floor plan becomes a home you can walk through, and a plan a contractor can quote from.**
The thing a viewer remembers: "I could see my flat before the renovation started."
The spine: one owner, one real flat (Blk 147A, 4-Room Type 1(H), about 90 m²), from the floor plan to the finished walkthrough.

Assumptions for all three: 35–45 s, 1920×1080 at 30 fps, warm instrumental music with sound effects on real actions, no voiceover or captions. Music comes from free local or royalty-free sources; paid catalog music is optional. Every screen is captured from the running app at localhost:5173, with no mock-ups.

---

## A. One take, plan to home (recommended: it's the whole product as one unbroken journey that anyone who's waited for a BTO understands)

**Device:** a moment in someone's day. **Form:** one continuous take.

A countdown reads "Key collection in 21 days" over the bare HDB plan. Then the camera never cuts: the plan extrudes into 3D, glides into the living room as walls are hacked and furniture lands, drops to eye height for the walkthrough at sunset, and finally rises out through the ceiling onto the budget and HDB checks. The viewer feels the flat go from a drawing to a place they could live in.

- **First 2 s:** the real black-and-white plan (`redhill-peaks-4rm-type1h.png`) fills the frame. A small chip at the top reads "Key collection in 21 days", and a pencil-thin terracotta line starts tracing the first wall.
- **Beats:** plan + countdown → trace draws itself, plan extrudes to 3D Orbit → Structural on, one wall hacked, glass study built, budget pill ticks → "Couple working from home" scheme drops furniture in → Japandi style washes over the room → sun slider to sunset, lights on → walkthrough from the living room into the main bedroom (payoff) → rise up to the report: S$ total against the S$60k budget, HDB checks pass → end card.
- **Look:** the app's own warm light-grey ground, white rounded panels, Inter, with the terracotta logo colour as the one accent (the traced line, the hacked wall, the end-card mark).
- **Sound:** soft felt-piano bed that opens up when the walkthrough begins; pencil scratch on the trace, a muted thud on the hack, a soft click per furniture drop, a light-switch tick at sunset.

## B. The wall

**Device:** life before and after, built around one decision. **Form:** split screen to one.

One question drives the film: "Should we hack the wall between the living room and Bedroom 3?" Two versions of the flat play side by side in the real **Compare** view, with their cameras moving in lockstep. The left keeps the wall; the right opens it up and gains a glass study. Each side earns its own budget and HDB result, then the split collapses into the version they choose, and we walk into it.

- **First 2 s:** a big cursor hovers one wall in 3D Orbit. It glows red ("can be hacked"), and the question types itself above it, ending on "?"
- **Beats:** the wall + the question → Structural view shows what can't move → Save preset "Keep the wall" → Hack, Build glass partition, Save preset "Open plan" → Compare: both 3D views orbit together → the two budget totals and HDB results land on each side → the split collapses into "Open plan" → walkthrough through the new opening (payoff) → end card.
- **Look:** a clean white split with a hairline divider; terracotta for the hacked wall, glass blue for the new partition, black for the decision.
- **Sound:** a two-note motif that plays in two voices while split and resolves into one when the screen collapses; a hack thud and a glass chime as the walls change.

## C. Same room, eight lives (the bold one)

**Device:** type carries the argument. **Form:** one frame that rebuilds, cutting on the beat.

The camera locks on the living room and never moves. On every beat the room becomes a different home: Japandi, Scandinavian, Muji, Modern Minimalist, Industrial, Modern Luxe, Wabi-sabi, Mid-century, with the style's name in giant Inter type behind the frame. Then the sun races from morning to night, the lights come on, and the camera finally lets go into the walkthrough.

- **First 2 s:** the furnished living room, still and centred. On the first downbeat it snaps from Japandi to Industrial, and the word "JAPANDI" is replaced by "INDUSTRIAL" in type taller than the room.
- **Beats:** eight style cuts on the beat → feature wall flips to fluted oak, ceiling to cove light → the sun slider sweeps the day to night → the camera breaks free to eye height, walkthrough (payoff) → a quick flash of the report: budget, shopping list, room board → end card.
- **Look:** each style's own palette swatches (taken from the app's style cards) as the full-bleed colour field behind the room, with heavy Inter in black.
- **Sound:** a tight, upbeat track at about 110 bpm; a fabric-swish swap on each cut, and the music opens out when the camera starts to move.
